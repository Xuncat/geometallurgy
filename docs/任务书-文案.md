## start
<!-- key: manual.geometallurgy. -->

**章标题**：工业化，开始！

### intro

**标题**：什么是虚拟矿脉

[l1] 为了存档安全和玩法方便起见，在加入本模组中后，地下不会生成任何真实的矿石方块。
[l2] 取而代之的是「虚拟矿脉」：按世界种子与区块坐标利用算法确定地算出来的一个矿脉。
[l3] 这样做的很多好处：地下不会到处是矿洞与矿石贴图，地形保持完整；同一片矿脉可以反复开采，不必满世界找新矿；矿体的形状、品位、伴生相都能照真实矿床的参数配，另外，还可以适配之后要加入的流动矿脉计划。
[l4] 本模组提供两种挖掘机：一种和原版接轨，挖掘原版矿物，一种则是特色，产出模组特色合金。挖掘机有两条线，见下面两条。

### solid

**标题**：第一种：原版式固体矿石

[l1] 第一条线是「矿脉抽取机」，它产出原版的矿石物品。
[l2] 矿脉抽取机的定位是「让本模组融进原版节奏」—— 不搭任何别的机器也能用，而且可以和原版材料联动。
[l3] 代价是它只给原版矿物，如果只使用这个机器，你无法玩到模组特色内容。

### fluid

**标题**：第二种：流动式混合矿浆

[l1] 第二条线是「流体碎矿挖掘机」，本模组的特色路线：它不产出矿石物品，而是把整块矿体打碎成矿块抽出，直接注入内部储罐。
[l2] 这份矿浆里含有模组特色成分 —— 主矿、脉石、伴生金属、杂质这些。
[l3] 之后的整条产线一直到出锭为止都在机器内部虚拟流体上跑：粉碎机磨细，磁力筛机按磁性分，离心机按密度分，最后才在冶炼炉里变回金属。
[l4] 这样做有个好处：不会导致堆叠灾难。
[l5] 这两条线不冲突：所有的基础机器仅仅需要原版材料，而这正是矿脉抽取机产出的东西。

### faces

**标题**：配置机器
<!-- 配方：engineering_configurator -->

[l1] 新摆下的机器，六个面全是关的 —— 它不会自己去抽料，也不会自己出货。哪个面接什么，需要玩家自己配置。
[l2] 配法一：右键机器打开界面，逐个面点成「进料 / 主产物 / 副产物 / 关闭」。
[l3] 配法二：工程配置器。Shift 右键机器复制它的六个面，再 Shift 右键另一台同类机器写进去；Shift 右键空气清空。
[l4] 只能同类复制：粉碎机对粉碎机、磁力筛机对磁力筛机。跨类型会被直接拒绝
[l5] 对于冶金炉，记得给它配好排渣口，否则跑到渣满它就会停在那里。

### prospect

**标题**：矿物探测仪
<!-- 配方：mineral_prospector -->

[l1] 模组矿脉不是方块，是埋在地下的「矿体」。每块矿体形状不同，有中心也有外沿：中心品位最高，越往外越低，边缘可能只剩中心的百分之几。
[l2] 短按右键后，选择界面打开，你可以用滚轮挑选要探的矿种。
[l3] 长按右键时 ，松开时扫描矿物，矿物探测仪会报出最近的该矿体中心坐标。

### generator

**标题**：火力发电机
<!-- 配方：coal_generator -->

[l1] 烧煤或木炭发电，把电推给相邻的机器。最基础的发电机，产出能量64FE/tick
[l2] 作用：烧煤、木炭或岩浆桶发电。输出 64 FE/t，内部缓冲 12800 FE，单刻最多外送 256 FE。
[l3] 本模组是「推电」模型：机器不会自己来取电，所以发电机必须挨着用电器，或者挨着已经通上电的机器。
[l4] 一台发电机大致够一台机器全速跑；要同时带多台，就多摆几台。

### drill

**标题**：钻头与挖掘
<!-- 配方：drill_bit_iron -->

[l1] 两种挖掘机：矿脉抽取机出固体矿石；流体碎矿挖掘机出混合矿浆。
[l2] 只有模组特色挖掘机需要钻头，钻头是耗材。
[l3] 钻头目前有四档：石 / 铁 / 金 / 钻石，之后会加入模组特色钻头。
[l4] 钻头硬度必须 ≥ 该处矿物的莫氏硬度，否则一个都挖不出来。韧性决定会不会当场崩碎。

### vein_extractor

**标题**：矿脉抽取机
<!-- 配方：vein_extractor -->

[l1] 作用：开采本区块的虚拟矿脉，产出原版矿石物品，共 9 个产物槽。
[l2] 耗电 256 FE/t，每 20 刻（1 秒）抽一次。要插钻头，且钻头硬度必须不低于该处矿物的莫氏硬度。
[l3] 越靠近矿体中心，单次产出越多。

### fluid_extractor

**标题**：流体碎矿挖掘机
<!-- 配方：fluid_vein_extractor -->

[l1] 作用：开采虚拟矿脉并磨成混合矿浆，注入内部储罐。耗电 256 FE/t，每 20 刻出 100 mB。
[l2] 同样要插钻头，硬度门槛与抽取机一致。
[l3] 它是整条流体线的源头：后面所有机器吃的都是它吐出来的这份矿浆。

### crusher

**标题**：粉碎机
<!-- 配方：crusher -->

[l1] 它会把矿浆或者固体磨碎。当输入为矿浆时，它会输出粉料，粉料进磁力筛机时，理想磁场窗口显著收窄；低纯度的粉在冶炼时产出的金属纯度会大打折扣。
[l2] 作用一 —— 把矿浆磨成矿粉，8 mB/t 的速度，耗能 8 ×（1 + 莫氏/4）FE/mB。成分原样照搬，纯度不涨。
[l3] 作用二 —— 正面两个物品槽磨整件东西：木炭 → 4 碳粉。进料槽只收能磨的，出料槽只出不进。
[l4] 两个槽也认六面配置：标「进」的面收料，标「出」的面出货，与矿浆的输入输出相同。
[l5] 两条路共用同一份电。矿粉进磁力筛机时理想磁场窗口会显著收窄，这是磨细的代价。

### separator

**标题**：磁力筛机
<!-- 配方：magnetic_separator -->

[l1] 按磁性把料分成精矿与尾矿。磁场强度由耗电决定：64 FE/t 对应额定磁场 1.0单位强度。
[l2] 磁场过弱时，磁性颗粒吸不住、漏进尾矿；磁场过强，非磁性颗粒被夹带进精矿。理想窗口是 0.5 ~ 1.5，也就是 输入电力32 ~ 96 FE/t。
[l3] 粉末物品经过干式磁选，而且有效磁场强度窗口更窄。
[l4] 作用：按磁性把料分成精矿与尾矿。磁场强度 = 耗电 ÷ 64，理想窗口 0.5 ~ 1.5，也就是 32 ~ 96 FE/t。
[l5] 磁场过弱，磁性颗粒吸不住、漏进尾矿；磁场过强，非磁性颗粒被夹带进精矿。
[l6] 矿浆与矿粉都收：粉走干式磁选，只是窗口更窄、更难调。

### centrifuge

**标题**：离心机
<!-- 配方：centrifuge -->

[l1] 离心机把可分离流体按密度分成重相与轻相。
[l2] 离心机只能离心流体。粉料颗粒太细，无法分层
[l3] 切点密度可在界面调，转速（功率）决定分离锐度：转速越低分离度越低，而切点决定在哪个百分比上的物质会被分离。
[l4] 作用：按密度把料分成重相与轻相，8 mB/t。切点可在界面调，范围 1.5 ~ 8.0，默认 3.0。
[l5] 只吃矿浆。粉料颗粒太细，液相带不住，没法沉降分层 —— 粉想分选只能走干式磁选。
[l6] 转速（功率）决定分离锐度，转速越低越分不清。

### routes

**标题**：三条路线

[l1] 走哪条路由矿物的化学性质决定，
[l2] 还原炉（碳热）是最通用的冶炼方法。氧化物矿粉进来，碳把氧夺走。试剂便宜、耗电中等，但是损失率与纯度都只是中等。
[l3] 电解炉（熔盐）—— 最贵也最干净。电耗是还原炉的十倍量级，换来最高的收率。活泼金属（铝、镁、钙）只能通过这种方式提出，只有两个电极均存在的时候才会运转。
[l4] 置换炉（湿法）—— 几乎不耗电。但每炼一单位金属就要消耗一单位更活泼的金属：副产物是盐溶液。
[l5] 选路线的例子：赤铁矿只能碳热还原；孔雀石还原或置换都行；萤石只能熔盐电解。
[l6] 提纯到高纯之后，矿物可以铸回原版或者其他模组的通用素材，接上兼容合成链。（待开发）

### reduction

**标题**：还原炉
<!-- 配方：reduction_furnace -->

[l1] 作用：碳热或金属热还原。每条配方自带功率：燃料驱动的传统火法（高炉 / 反射炉 / 自然金属熔炼）一度电都不吃，电炉与电弧那一路基准 80 FE/t、上限 192；一个炉次 100 刻。
[l2] 辅助剂放碳系（煤炭 / 木炭 / 碳粉 / 碳棒）就行，永远通路；金属只是可选的替代，不强制。只有置换炉才必须用金属，且要比目标活泼。
[l3] 最通用的一台，收率与纯度都居中；赤铁矿这类氧化物矿只能走它。

### electrolysis

**标题**：电解炉
<!-- 配方：electrolysis_furnace -->

[l1] 作用：熔融电解。基准 80 FE/t、一个炉次 120 刻，但电解配方的电耗倍率很高，实际开销是还原炉的十倍量级。
[l2] 必须阳极 + 阴极成对才开得起来，两支电极各自按耐久消耗：碳棒便宜只撑 8 个炉次，钨棒 64、钼棒 40。
[l3] 活泼金属（铝、镁、钙）与萤石只有这一条路；换来的是三条路线里最高的收率。

### displacement

**标题**：置换炉
<!-- 配方：displacement_furnace -->

[l1] 作用：湿法置换。不接电（0 FE/t），一个炉次 60 刻，代价是吃掉一种比目标更活泼的金属。
[l2] 代价是每炼一份金属就要吃掉一份更活泼的金属（铁顶铜、锌顶银），并副产一罐盐液。
[l3] 手里正好有便宜活泼金属的时候最划算。

### electrode

**标题**：电极
<!-- 配方：carbon_rod -->

[l1] 电解炉的电极是消耗品，一根撑若干个炉次。
[l2] 碳棒，最便宜的电极，只撑 8 个炉次。
[l3] 钨棒 / 钼棒 钨棒撑 64 个炉次、钼棒 40 个，长期跑电解靠它们。

### casting

**标题**：铸造

[l1] 高纯锭可以铸成原版锭，接上原版合成链。
[l2] 门槛是主金属 ≥ 90%，也就是名字里带「精」的那种。不到 90% 铸不出来 —— 杂质太多，铸出来的东西不能当那种金属用。
[l3] 一般来说，主金属 ≥ 70% 叫主金属的锭；不到 70% 一律叫「合金锭」。
[l4] 合金锭目前只能当材料，还没有专门用途。

### battery

**标题**：蓄电池
<!-- 配方：battery -->

[l1] 19×19×19 范围内的无线电网中心，供电损耗50%，输电额外损耗50%，损耗降低升级模块待开发。
[l2] 两个页签就是两种模式：输入页从电源设备受电，只列能供电的设备；输出页向用电设备供电，只列能受电的设备。
[l3] 列表里每台设备都有自己的功率档，用 − / + 循环调，0 档就是停供。单次传输上限 64 FE/t。
[l4] 作用：19×19×19 范围内无线电网的中心。损耗 50%，送达 1 点要花 2 点；单次传输上限 64 FE/t。
[l5] 界面两个页签就是两种模式：输入页只列能供电的设备，输出页只列能受电的设备。
[l6] 每台设备有独立的功率档（− / + 循环，0 档停供），最多 16 台，列表可用滚轮翻看。

### sensor

**标题**：无线感应器
<!-- 配方：wireless_sensor -->

[l1] 无线感应器立在机器顶面，它是这台机器的「入网许可」。没装感应器的机器，蓄电池无法连接。
[l2] 作用：立在机器顶面，作为那台机器的「入网许可」。没装感应器的机器，蓄电池看不见它。
[l3] 它是寄生方块：宿主机被拆掉，它跟着碎，不会留下指向空气的感应器。
[l4] 一台机器一个就够。


## guns
<!-- key: manual.geometallurgy_guns. -->

**章标题**：枪械（联动）

### bench

**标题**：枪械合成台
<!-- 配方：gun_bench -->

[l1] 先造这台台子。它用原版材料就能做，不必先用冶金机器攒料：6 铁锭 + 1 铁块 + 1 工作台，摆成下面这个样子。
[l2] 摆好后右键打开。台子是「锭 → 枪 / 配件 / 子弹」的唯一入口，后面几页说的东西都从这里出。
[l3] 台子挖掉时会把里面的料吐出来，不会吞东西。

### modes

**标题**：三种模式，每排都能选

[l1] 界面顶上有一排模式按钮，永远都在：造枪 / 造配件 / 造子弹。点一下就换一套槽位。
[l2] 模式下面还有一排（造子弹时是两排）选项按钮，用来选具体做哪一种：造枪选枪型，造配件选做法，造子弹选弹种，再选装药规格。
[l3] 切换任何一排都会重摆槽位，但界面不会关掉重开 —— 鼠标不会被拽回屏幕中央，可以连着点。
[l4] 槽位永远按最宽的那种做法留着。切到槽少的做法时，多出来的格子只是不显示、不可点，里面的东西还在，切回来照旧。
[l5] 扣料发生在你从产物格取走东西的那一刻。料摆错了只是没有产物，不会被静默吃掉。

### gun

**标题**：造枪：空枪体

[l1] 造枪模式：10 块金属锭 + 4 个任意木头 → 一把空枪体。木头什么种类都行。
[l2] 「金属锭」指本模组任意金属锭 —— 铁锭、钢锭、合金锭都算，不限于某一种。
[l3] 枪型在第一排选：AK-47 或 GM 步枪。两种枪型的用料完全相同，差别在枪本身的定位，不在料。
[l4] 拿到手的是空枪体：没有枪管、没有撞针，所以打不响。这不是漏了，枪管与撞针是必须另做另装的配件。
[l5] 枪体不承担性能。它只记「自己是哪块料做的」这个身份；性能由装上来的枪管决定，稀土由撞针决定 —— 各管各的。

### barrel

**标题**：配件之一：枪管

[l1] 造配件模式里选「枪管」：3 块金属锭 → 1 根枪管。
[l2] 枪管决定这把枪的耐久、威力阈值、后坐与连发阈值 —— 换句话说，性能几乎全在它身上。
[l3] 所以用哪块料做枪管很要紧：料的成分直接算进这几项。同样的枪身，配不同料的枪管就是两把不同的枪。

### pin

**标题**：配件之二：稀土撞针

[l1] 造配件模式里选「稀土撞针」：1 块金属锭 + 1 个铁粒 → 1 根稀土撞针。
[l2] 这块料有门槛：稀土要占 15% 以上才做得成。达不到就不给产物，料还在槽里。
[l3] 稀土撞针是好的那条路：激发率 90~99%，寿命数百发。数值来自那块合金的成分。
[l4] 电火花撞针不在配方里 —— 它要更复杂的工序，这一版还做不出来。

### flint

**标题**：配件之三：燧石撞针

[l1] 造配件模式里选「燧石撞针」：1 个燧石 + 1 个铁粒 → 1 根燧石撞针。
[l2] 它不判成分 —— 燧石不是金属，没有那套数据，所以它的数值是三条定值：激发 82%、寿命 120 发，比稀土撞针更容易崩。
[l3] 它给出一条低门槛的路：稀土是稀缺资源，前期几乎做不出能打响的枪。燧石撞针让你前期就有一把能用的枪，代价是性能全线更差。攒到稀土再换。

### ammo

**标题**：造子弹：弹种与发数

[l1] 造子弹模式：1 块金属 + 若干火药 → 一批子弹。第一排选弹种，第二排选装药规格。
[l2] 弹种决定一块金属出几发：7.62x39 一锭出 10 发（短壳，26.8mm）；7.92x57 一锭出 7 发（长壳，35.0mm）。
[l3] 长弹更费料，换来的优势在弹道上 —— 那是拿料换的，不是白拿的。
[l4] 一次合成恰好吃掉 1 块金属。想做多批就多点几次，每次的产出是定值，界面上的产物数量不会随槽里的余料漂移。

### charge

**标题**：装药规格：自选，不是猜的

[l1] 第二排是装药规格，三选一，各自吃不同数量的火药。
[l2] 装药 I：吃 1 颗火药，短壳，初速 ×0.80，炸膛伤 20 血。
[l3] 装药 II：吃 2 颗火药，中壳，初速 ×1.00，炸膛伤 25 血。
[l4] 装药 III：吃 3 颗火药，长壳，初速 ×1.25，炸膛伤 30 血。
[l5] 装药越多，打出去的初速越高，但枪一旦炸膛，你自己也越疼。
[l6] 规格不改发数，只改装药：发数由弹种决定，装药由规格决定，两根轴各管一头。

### assemble

**标题**：装齐才能开火

[l1] 枪管与撞针都做出来之后，把它们装到枪上。手上拿着这把枪，按 Z 键打开组装界面。
[l2] 枪管与撞针是必选件，缺一个都打不响 —— 界面上会提示「必选部件未装齐」。
[l3] 两件都装齐之后，拿到手就能直接开火。
[l4] 打久了枪管与撞针都会磨损，磨到 100% 就报废。撞针还会在寿命未尽时按断裂率当场断掉。

## start
<!-- key: manual.geometallurgy. -->

**章标题**：Getting Started

### intro

**标题**：What a Virtual Vein Is

[l1] This mod generates no real ore blocks underground.
[l2] Instead there are virtual veins: a body of ore computed deterministically from the world seed and chunk coordinates. It exists only as a calculation and occupies no block.
[l3] Three benefits: the underground stays intact instead of being riddled with ore textures; the same vein can be mined again and again instead of hunting for new ones; and shape, grade and companion phases can follow real deposit parameters.
[l4] Find veins with the Mineral Prospector and mine them with an extractor. Extractors come in two routes, described below.

### solid

**标题**：Route one: solid ore items

[l1] The first route is the Vein Extractor, which follows the vanilla convention: it outputs vanilla ore items.
[l2] Its job is to let this mod fit the vanilla pace - it works with no other machine from this mod, and can hook into vanilla materials.
[l3] The price is that it only gives vanilla minerals: use this machine alone and you never reach the mod's own content.

### fluid

**标题**：Route two: mixed slurry

[l1] The second route is the Fluid Vein Crusher, this mod's own line: it yields no ore items at all, grinding the whole body into a suspension and piping it into an internal tank.
[l2] That slurry carries every component of the ore - main mineral, gangue and companion traces, nothing left out.
[l3] From there the whole chain runs on fluid: the crusher grinds it finer, the separator splits by magnetism, the centrifuge splits by density, and only then does a smelter turn it back into metal.
[l4] The payoff is that the hard parts of real ore - grade, grain size, impurities, companion phases - all have somewhere to live.
[l5] The two routes do not conflict: mine solid ore for a quick start, then move over to the fluid line.

### faces

**标题**：Configuring the six faces
<!-- 配方：engineering_configurator -->

[l1] A freshly placed machine has all six faces closed - it will not pull anything in, nor push anything out. Which face does what is your call.
[l2] Way one: right-click the machine, then click each face through input / output / byproduct / disabled.
[l3] Way two: the Engineering Configurator (1 iron ingot + 2 redstone + 1 paper). Shift-right-click a machine to copy its six faces, then Shift-right-click another machine of the same type to write them in; Shift-right-click air to clear.
[l4] Copying only works between machines of the same type - crusher to crusher, magnetic separator to magnetic separator. Crossing types is refused outright, because the two face-role enums number their values differently; forcing it would only give you a machine with six wrong faces.
[l5] One reminder: give a smelter its slag port first, or the first batch will run until the slag tank is full and stop there.

### prospect

**标题**：Mineral Prospector
<!-- 配方：mineral_prospector -->

[l1] Veins are not blocks - they are virtual ore bodies in the ground. Each body has a core and a rim: grade is highest at the core, and falls off outward, sometimes to a few percent of the core value.
[l2] Short right-click opens a picker; scroll to choose the mineral you want to prospect for.
[l3] Long right-click scans on release and reports the nearest ore-body core of that mineral, with its bearing, distance and depth.

### generator

**标题**：Coal Generator
<!-- 配方：coal_generator -->

[l1] Burns coal or charcoal to generate power and push it to neighbouring machines - the entry-level generator, outputting 64 FE/t.
[l2] It burns coal, charcoal or lava buckets. Output 64 FE/t, internal buffer 12800 FE, up to 256 FE pushed out per tick.
[l3] This mod pushes power rather than letting machines pull it: machines never reach out for power, so a generator must touch its consumer, or touch a machine that is already powered.
[l4] One generator roughly runs one machine flat out; add more to run more at once.

### drill

**标题**：Drill Bits and Extractors
<!-- 配方：drill_bit_iron -->

[l1] Two extractors: the Vein Extractor yields solid ore items, the Fluid Vein Crusher yields mixed slurry.
[l2] Both need a drill bit, and bits are consumables.
[l3] Four tiers: stone / iron / gold / diamond.
[l4] Hardness decides what it can bite: the bit hardness must be at least the mineral Mohs value, otherwise nothing comes out (the status shows bit too soft). Toughness decides whether it shatters, and durability is wear - so diamond is the hardest and the shortest-lived.

### vein_extractor

**标题**：Vein Extractor
<!-- 配方：vein_extractor -->

[l1] Role: mines the chunk's virtual vein and yields vanilla ore items, into 9 output slots.
[l2] Draws 256 FE/t and harvests once every 20 ticks. Needs a drill bit whose hardness is at least the mineral's Mohs value.
[l3] The closer to the vein core, the more each harvest yields.

### fluid_extractor

**标题**：Fluid Vein Crusher
<!-- 配方：fluid_vein_extractor -->

[l1] Role: mines virtual veins and grinds them into mixed slurry in an internal tank. Draws 256 FE/t and yields 100 mB every 20 ticks.
[l2] It also needs a drill bit, with the same hardness threshold as the solid extractor.
[l3] It is the head of the entire fluid chain: every later machine eats the slurry it produces.

### crusher

**标题**：Crusher
<!-- 配方：crusher -->

[l1] It grinds slurry or solids down. On slurry input it outputs powder; when that powder later reaches the magnetic separator its ideal field window narrows sharply, and low-grade powder yields far lower metal purity when smelted.
[l2] Job one - grinds slurry into powder at 8 mB/t, costing 8 x (1 + Mohs/4) FE per mB. Composition is carried over unchanged and purity does not rise.
[l3] Job two - two item slots on its face grind whole items: charcoal to 4 carbon powder. The input slot only takes grindables; the output only gives.
[l4] The two slots also obey the six-face roles: faces marked in feed the input, faces marked out give the output, the same setting the slurry uses.
[l5] Both jobs share the same power. Powder narrows the separator's ideal field window - that is the price of grinding finer.

### separator

**标题**：Magnetic Separator
<!-- 配方：magnetic_separator -->

[l1] Splits feed into concentrate and tailings by magnetism. Field strength follows draw: 64 FE/t is nominal field 1.0.
[l2] Both extremes fail: too weak and magnetic grains are not held and slip to tailings; too strong and non-magnetic grains are dragged into concentrate. The ideal window is 0.5 to 1.5, i.e. 32 to 96 FE/t.
[l3] Powder runs as dry magnetic separation, with a narrower ideal field window.
[l4] Role: splits feed into concentrate and tailings by magnetism. Field equals draw divided by 64; the ideal window is 0.5 to 1.5, i.e. 32 to 96 FE/t.
[l5] Too weak and magnetic grains slip into the tailings; too strong and non-magnetic grains are dragged into the concentrate.
[l6] It takes slurry and powder alike; powder runs dry separation with a narrower, fiddlier window.

### centrifuge

**标题**：Centrifuge
<!-- 配方：centrifuge -->

[l1] Splits feed into heavy and light phases by density.
[l2] Centrifuge only takes fluid. Powder particles are too fine to be carried and cannot settle.
[l3] The cut density is adjustable in the GUI, and spin (power) sets sharpness: slower means blurrier. The cut decides which percentage splits off at what point.
[l4] Role: splits feed into heavy and light phases at 8 mB/t. The cut is adjustable in the GUI from 1.5 to 8.0, default 3.0.
[l5] Slurry only. Powder particles are too fine for the liquid phase to carry and cannot settle out - powder must go through dry magnetic separation.
[l6] Spin (power) sets sharpness; slower means blurrier.

### routes

**标题**：Three Routes

[l1] The route is decided by the mineral chemistry, not by a fixed chain.
[l2] Reduction Furnace (carbothermic) - the generalist. 4 iron blocks + 4 bricks + 1 furnace + 1 coal block. Oxide powder goes in and carbon takes the oxygen. Cheap reagent, medium power, medium yield and grade.
[l3] Electrolysis Furnace (molten salt) - expensive and clean. 4 copper blocks + 2 redstone blocks + 2 iron blocks + 1 lightning rod. About ten times the power of reduction for the highest yield. Reactive metals (aluminium, magnesium, calcium) only go this way. An anode and a cathode pair are required, each wearing separately.
[l4] Displacement Furnace (wet) - almost no power. 4 copper blocks + 2 glass + 2 iron blocks + 1 furnace. But every unit of metal eats a unit of a more reactive metal: iron for copper, zinc for silver. Yields a tank of brine.
[l5] Picking a route: hematite is carbothermic only; malachite takes reduction or displacement; fluorite only goes through molten electrolysis.
[l6] High-purity ingots can be cast back into vanilla ingots to rejoin vanilla crafting.

### reduction

**标题**：Reduction Furnace
<!-- 配方：reduction_furnace -->

[l1] Role: carbothermic or metallothermic reduction. 40 FE/t base, 100 ticks per batch.
[l2] The reagent can be carbon-based (general purpose) or a metal more reactive than the target.
[l3] The generalist of the three, with middling yield and grade; oxide ores such as hematite can only go this way.

### electrolysis

**标题**：Electrolysis Furnace
<!-- 配方：electrolysis_furnace -->

[l1] Role: molten electrolysis. 80 FE/t base and 120 ticks per batch, but electrolysis recipes carry a high power multiplier, so the real drain is about ten times reduction.
[l2] It needs an anode and a cathode pair, each wearing down separately: a carbon rod is cheap but lasts 8 batches, tungsten 64, molybdenum 40.
[l3] Reactive metals (aluminium, magnesium, calcium) and fluorite only go this way, in exchange for the highest yield of the three.

### displacement

**标题**：Displacement Furnace
<!-- 配方：displacement_furnace -->

[l1] Role: wet displacement. Only 16 FE/t, 60 ticks per batch - the cheapest of the three routes to run.
[l2] The price is that every unit of metal eats a unit of a more reactive metal (iron for copper, zinc for silver) and yields a tank of brine.
[l3] Best when you happen to have cheap reactive metal on hand.

### electrode

**标题**：Electrodes
<!-- 配方：carbon_rod -->

[l1] Electrodes are consumables; one rod lasts a number of batches.
[l2] Carbon rod - the cheapest electrode, lasting 8 batches.
[l3] Tungsten / molybdenum rod - tungsten lasts 64 batches, molybdenum 40; long electrolysis runs depend on them.

### casting

**标题**：Casting

[l1] High-purity ingots can be cast back into vanilla ingots to rejoin vanilla crafting.
[l2] The bar is 90% base metal - the ones named fine. Below that you cannot cast, because too much impurity means the result could not honestly be called that metal.
[l3] 70% base metal is the threshold to be named after it; below that it is simply an alloy ingot.
[l4] Alloy ingots are material only for now, with no special use yet.

### battery

**标题**：Battery
<!-- 配方：battery -->

[l1] The hub of a 19x19x19 wireless grid, with 50% loss: delivering 1 costs 2.
[l2] The two tabs are the two modes: the input tab draws from power sources and lists only extractable devices; the output tab feeds consumers and lists only insertable devices.
[l3] Each listed device has its own rate, cycled with - and +; rate 0 means no supply. Transfer is capped at 64 FE/t.
[l4] Role: the hub of a 19x19x19 wireless grid. 50% loss - delivering 1 costs 2 - with a 64 FE/t transfer cap.
[l5] The two tabs are the two modes: the input tab lists only extractable devices, the output tab only insertable ones.
[l6] Each device has its own rate (cycled with - and +, 0 means no supply), up to 16 devices, scrollable with the wheel.

### sensor

**标题**：Wireless Sensor
<!-- 配方：wireless_sensor -->

[l1] Stand it on top of a machine to grant that machine grid access. Without a sensor the battery simply cannot see the machine.
[l2] Role: stands on top of a machine as that machine's grid permit. Without one, the battery cannot see the machine.
[l3] It is parasitic: break the host and it shatters with it, so no dangling sensor is ever left pointing at a machine that is gone.
[l4] One sensor per machine is enough.


## guns
<!-- key: manual.geometallurgy_guns. -->

**章标题**：Guns (Add-on)

### bench

**标题**：Gun Bench
<!-- 配方：gun_bench -->

[l1] Build the bench first. It uses vanilla materials only, so you do not need any metallurgy machines to make it: 6 iron ingots + 1 iron block + 1 crafting table, arranged as shown below.
[l2] Right-click it to open. The bench is the only entry point from ingots to guns, parts and ammo - everything in the following pages comes out of it.
[l3] Breaking the bench spits its contents back out. Nothing is swallowed.

### modes

**标题**：Three Modes, Every Row Selectable

[l1] The top row of buttons is always there: Gun / Parts / Ammo. One click swaps the whole slot layout.
[l2] Below it sits one more row of option buttons (two rows for ammo) to pick the exact recipe: gun model for guns, method for parts, calibre and charge for ammo.
[l3] Switching any row re-lays the slots, but the screen is never closed and reopened - the mouse is not yanked back to the centre, so you can keep clicking.
[l4] Slots are always sized for the widest method. When you switch to a narrower one, the extra slots are merely hidden and inert - whatever sits inside them stays there and comes back when you switch back.
[l5] Ingredients are consumed at the moment you take the result out. A wrong layout simply yields nothing; it is never silently eaten.

### gun

**标题**：Guns: The Bare Receiver

[l1] Gun mode: 10 metal ingots + 4 of any wood -> one bare receiver. Any wood type works.
[l2] "Metal ingot" means any ingot from this mod - iron, steel and alloy all count, not just one specific kind.
[l3] Pick the model on the first row: AK-47 or GM Rifle. Both cost exactly the same; the difference is the gun's role, not the material.
[l4] What you get is a bare receiver: no barrel, no firing pin, so it will not fire. That is not an oversight - the barrel and pin are mandatory parts you craft and fit separately.
[l5] The receiver carries no performance. It only remembers which metal it was made from; performance comes from the barrel you fit, and the rare earth from the pin - each part minds its own business.

### barrel

**标题**：Part 1: Barrel

[l1] In Parts mode pick "Barrel": 3 metal ingots -> 1 barrel.
[l2] The barrel sets this gun's durability, power limit, recoil and heat limit - in other words, almost all of its performance.
[l3] So which metal you use matters a lot: its composition feeds straight into those stats. Same receiver plus a different barrel is a different gun.

### pin

**标题**：Part 2: Rare-Earth Firing Pin

[l1] In Parts mode pick "Rare-Earth Pin": 1 metal ingot + 1 iron nugget -> 1 rare-earth firing pin.
[l2] That metal has a threshold: rare earth must make up at least 15%. Below that there is no result, and the materials stay in the slots.
[l3] The rare-earth pin is the good path: 90-99% ignition, hundreds of rounds of life. Its numbers come from the alloy's composition.
[l4] The spark pin is not in the recipes - it needs a more involved process that this version does not provide yet.

### flint

**标题**：Part 3: Flint Firing Pin

[l1] In Parts mode pick "Flint Pin": 1 flint + 1 iron nugget -> 1 flint firing pin.
[l2] It ignores composition - flint is not a metal and has none of that data, so its stats are three fixed values: 82% ignition, 120 rounds of life, and a higher chance to snap than the rare-earth pin.
[l3] It offers a low bar to entry: rare earth is scarce, so early on you can barely build a gun that fires at all. The flint pin gets you a working gun early, at the cost of worse stats across the board. Swap it out once you have rare earth.

### ammo

**标题**：Ammo: Calibre and Yield

[l1] Ammo mode: 1 metal + some gunpowder -> one batch of rounds. The first row picks the calibre, the second picks the charge grade.
[l2] The calibre sets how many rounds one ingot yields: 7.62x39 gives 10 rounds per ingot (short case, 26.8mm); 7.92x57 gives 7 (long case, 35.0mm).
[l3] Long rounds cost more material, and what they buy is ballistics - paid for, not free.
[l4] One craft consumes exactly 1 metal. Want more batches? Click more times. Each craft yields a fixed amount, so the result count on screen never drifts with leftovers in the slots.

### charge

**标题**：Charge Grade: Your Choice, Not a Guess

[l1] The second row is the charge grade, one of three, each eating a different amount of gunpowder.
[l2] Charge I: 1 gunpowder, short case, muzzle velocity x0.80, kaboom damage 20 HP.
[l3] Charge II: 2 gunpowder, medium case, muzzle velocity x1.00, kaboom damage 25 HP.
[l4] Charge III: 3 gunpowder, long case, muzzle velocity x1.25, kaboom damage 30 HP.
[l5] More charge means higher muzzle velocity - but if the gun blows up, it hurts you more too.
[l6] The grade does not change the round count, only the charge: count comes from the calibre, charge from the grade. Each axis minds its own end.

### assemble

**标题**：Fit Everything Before Firing

[l1] Once you have both a barrel and a pin, fit them onto the gun. Hold the gun and press Z to open the assembly screen.
[l2] The barrel and pin are mandatory; missing either one and the gun will not fire - the screen says "mandatory parts missing".
[l3] With both fitted, it fires straight from your hand.
[l4] Both barrel and pin wear with use; at 100% wear they are scrapped. The pin can also snap outright, at its snap rate, before its life runs out.
